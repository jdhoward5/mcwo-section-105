### Data
Information in a specific representation, usually as a sequence of symbols that have meaning.  
**Sources:**  
[CNSSI 4009-2015](https://www.cnss.gov/CNSS/issuances/Instructions.cfm) from [IETF RFC 4949 Ver 2](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc4949)
### Information
Any communication or representation of knowledge such as facts, data, or opinions in any medium or form, including textual, numerical, graphic, cartographic, narrative, or audiovisual. An instance of an information type.  
**Sources:**  
[NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-30r1) under Information from CNSSI 4009, [FIPS 199](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.FIPS.199)

### Protocol
A set of rules (i.e., formats and procedures) to implement and control some type of association (e.g., communication) between systems.
